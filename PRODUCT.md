# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Kao Mai primarily serves shelter and NGO staff who manage resident profiles and coordinate employment, and employers who create job opportunities, review suitable candidates, and confirm hires. The public-facing website also serves prospective partners, employers, donors, and supporters who need to understand the mission, see credible evidence of impact, and find a clear way to participate.

Users work across a range of devices and levels of digital confidence. The initial experience is Thai-first and should help each audience complete important tasks with minimal ambiguity. The architecture should allow English localization later without major restructuring.

## Product Purpose

Kao Mai connects people experiencing homelessness with dignified employment opportunities through collaboration between shelters, NGOs, and employers. The operational platform exists to make resident management, job posting, candidate matching, and hiring coordination reliable and efficient. The public website exists to explain Kao Mai's mission, demonstrate impact, attract partners and donors, and guide qualified users into the platform.

Success means people can rebuild independence through employment, staff can coordinate opportunities without unnecessary administrative friction, employers can hire with confidence, and supporters can understand and trust Kao Mai's impact.

## Brand Personality

Dignified, empowering, hopeful.

The experience should feel professional, trustworthy, inclusive, optimistic, and human-centered. It should frame people through their abilities, agency, and potential rather than through pity. Its warmth should come from respectful language, real outcomes, approachable interactions, and considered visual choices.

## Anti-references

- Bureaucratic government portals with dense content, outdated presentation, or intimidating flows.
- Sentimental charity campaigns that center pity, helplessness, or emotional manipulation.
- Generic corporate SaaS dashboards that feel interchangeable, sterile, or detached from Kao Mai's mission.
- Overly playful interfaces or cartoonish illustrations that undermine dignity and professionalism.
- Visual imitation of Airbnb, Notion, or GOV.UK; their useful qualities should inform principles, not become a copied aesthetic.

## Design Principles

1. **Make dignity visible.** Present every person as capable and active in rebuilding their independence; use respectful language and imagery throughout.
2. **Make the next action obvious.** Borrow the task clarity of GOV.UK and the hierarchy of Notion so users can complete operational work confidently and efficiently.
3. **Balance warmth with professional trust.** Use approachable composition and thoughtful whitespace without sacrificing the reliability expected from employment and case-management workflows.
4. **Show credible impact.** Communicate outcomes with clear evidence, real stories, and transparent context rather than emotional pressure or decorative statistics.
5. **Design one coherent ecosystem.** The public website and authenticated platform should share a recognizable identity while adapting their hierarchy to different user goals.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 Level AA across the public website and operational platform. Support complete keyboard operation, visible focus states, semantic structure, sufficient color contrast, accessible forms and validation, screen-reader-compatible status messaging, reduced-motion preferences, and layouts that remain usable under text zoom and responsive reflow.

Thai is the primary language for the MVP. Content, routing, components, formatting, and data structures should be designed for future English localization without requiring major refactoring. Avoid language or interaction patterns that stigmatize people experiencing homelessness or assume high digital literacy.
